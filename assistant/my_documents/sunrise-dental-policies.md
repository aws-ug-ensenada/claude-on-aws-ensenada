# Sunrise Dental — clinic policies

*Fictional sample data for the Luna demo.*

## Appointments
- Reminders go out 48 hours and 4 hours before the visit. A patient who has not confirmed 4 hours before is called by reception.
- Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are charged 20% of the treatment price. For example, a patient who cancels 5 hours before the visit is inside the 24-hour window and is charged 20%; one who cancels 2 days before is not charged. Whitening deposits (500 MXN) are not refundable inside 24 hours.
- New patients arrive 15 minutes early to fill in the medical history form.

## Medical alerts
- Always check the notes column for allergies (penicillin, latex) before the visit and tell the dentist at the morning huddle.
- Anxious patients get a 30-minute buffer after the booked slot.

## Insurance
- Accepted: Seguros Pacífico and Salud Norte. Patients pay the deductible on the day; the clinic files the claim within 5 working days.

## Sterilisation
- Instruments are sterilised in sealed pouches. Keep at least one box of pouches above the reorder point at all times.
