# Bodega Cielo Alto — team handbook

*Fictional sample business for the Luna demo: a winery with a tasting room and restaurant in Valle de Guadalupe, Ensenada.*

## Opening hours

- **Tasting room:** Thursday to Sunday, 11:00–18:00. Last tasting starts at 17:15.
- **Restaurant:** Thursday to Sunday, 13:00–21:00. Kitchen closes at 20:30.
- Closed Monday to Wednesday, except private events booked in advance.

## Tastings and prices

| Experience | Price (MXN) | Price (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic tasting (4 wines) | 450 | 25 | Walk-ins welcome until 16:00 |
| Reserve tasting (6 wines + cheese board) | 850 | 48 | Reservation required |
| Vineyard tour + tasting | 1,200 | 68 | Saturdays 11:00 and 15:00, max 12 people |

We accept US dollars at the day's reference exchange rate, rounded down to the nearest peso in the guest's favour.

## Reservations

- Groups of **8 or more** need a **30% deposit**.
- Free cancellation up to **48 hours** before; after that the deposit is kept.
- Ask every group about **allergies and dietary needs** when confirming.

## Patio rules

- The patio **closes** when the forecast shows wind above **30 km/h** or a chance of rain above **60%**. Move bookings indoors (40 seats inside, 60 on the patio).
- Heaters go on when the low is below **12 °C**.

## Wine club

- Members get 15% off bottles and two free classic tastings a year.
- Shipping to the US is **not** allowed from the winery; members pick up in person.

## Staff

- Weekend shifts: tasting room 10:30–18:30 (3 people), restaurant 12:30–21:30 (5 people).
- The shift lead closes the till and sends the day's totals to the owner before 22:00.
