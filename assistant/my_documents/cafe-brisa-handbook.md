# Café Brisa — team handbook

*Fictional sample business for the Luna demo: a coffee shop on the Ensenada malecón.*

## Hours and shifts

- Open every day, **07:00–19:00**.
- Morning shift 06:30–13:00 (2 baristas), afternoon shift 12:30–19:30 (2 baristas). Saturdays and Sundays add a third barista from 09:00 to 15:00.
- Cruise-ship days (check the port schedule) add a third barista on weekdays too.

## Menu and prices (MXN)

| Drink | 12 oz | 16 oz |
|---|---|---|
| Americano | 45 | 55 |
| Latte / Cappuccino | 60 | 72 |
| Cold brew | 65 | 78 |
| Pumpkin latte (seasonal) | 75 | 88 |

Oat milk adds 12 pesos. US dollars are accepted at the day's reference rate.

## Recipes

- **Latte 16 oz:** double shot (18 g espresso beans), 300 ml milk.
- **Cold brew:** 1 kg filter beans makes 8 litres of concentrate, about 32 drinks. Steep 18 hours.

## Loyalty card

The tenth drink is free. The card can't be combined with other promotions.

## Opening and closing

- Opening: calibrate the grinder, run two test shots, check milk dates.
- Closing: clean the steam wands, empty the grounds bin, count the till and send the totals to the owner before 20:00.
