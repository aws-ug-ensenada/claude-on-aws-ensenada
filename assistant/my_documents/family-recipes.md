## Grandma's fish tacos (tacos de pescado estilo Ensenada)

Serves 4 · 30 minutes

**Ingredients:** 500 g white fish (cazón or tilapia) in strips · 1 cup flour · 1 cup cold beer (or sparkling water) · 1 tsp mustard · salt and pepper · oil for frying · 12 corn tortillas · shredded cabbage · crema · pico de gallo · limes.

1. Mix flour, beer, mustard, salt and pepper into a thick batter. Rest it 10 minutes.
2. Dry the fish, dip it in the batter and fry at 180 °C for 3–4 minutes until golden.
3. Serve on warm tortillas with cabbage, crema thinned with lime juice, and pico de gallo.

**Grandma's secret:** a pinch of oregano in the batter, and the beer must be very cold.

## Vegetarian option: cauliflower "fish" tacos

Use the same batter with cauliflower florets. Fry 5–6 minutes. Everything else stays the same.
